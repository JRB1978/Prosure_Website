Pipe Schedule Reference

Outside diameter, wall thickness, internal diameter, and weight for standard nominal pipe sizes, by schedule. Internal diameter and weight are calculated from the outside diameter and wall thickness.

NPS DN OD (mm) Wall Thickness (mm) ID (mm) Weight (kg/m)

For reference only. Dimensions follow ASME B36.10M, with the Schedule 5 values and the Schedule 10 values up to NPS 12 taken from the stainless 5S / 10S series in B36.19M; they were cross-checked against published reference tables in November 2026. Weights are plain-end carbon steel, from the mass formula in B36.10M. “Not tabulated” means the standard does not list that schedule at that size. Please confirm against the current edition of the standard and vendor data before detailed design or procurement.
