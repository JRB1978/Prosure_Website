About

An independent consultancy, since 2018

Prosure is a privately owned and managed consultancy specialising in expert process engineering and flow assurance services across the energy sector. Founded in 2018, we've completed over 50 projects globally for clients in oil & gas, carbon capture and storage, and hydrogen generation — spanning appraisal, front-end, detailed, and operational phases of design.

Our experience across a wide range of projects has given us a thorough appreciation of the risks and uncertainties that shape the design process. Through that experience, and the clever application of industry-standard software and bespoke in-house tools, we deliver robust, practical life-of-field engineering solutions tailored to your system.
